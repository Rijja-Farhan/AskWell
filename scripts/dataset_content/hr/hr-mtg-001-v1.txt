## Meeting Details

Date: 2025-09-18. Attendees: Jordan Price (HR Director), Priya Shah (HR Business Partner), Leo Brandt (Finance Controller), Ava Chen (Operations Manager). Notes taken by Priya Shah. Confidential: do not forward outside the attendee list.

## Merit and Promotion Budgets for 2026

The committee agreed a merit increase budget of 3.5% of base payroll for 2026, down from 4.0% in 2025. A separate promotion budget of 1.0% of base payroll will be held centrally by HR. Managers will receive their merit worksheets on 2025-11-03 and must return them by 2025-11-21. Increases take effect on the first payroll in January 2026.

Leo noted that the 2026 budget assumes merit spend stays within 3.5%. Any department above this needs CFO approval.

## Warehouse Starting Wages

Ava presented turnover data for the Dallas sites. First-year turnover for warehouse associates at WH-DAL-01 and WH-DAL-02 reached 48% in the last twelve months. Exit interviews mention pay as the top reason.

Decision: the starting hourly wage for warehouse associates at both Dallas sites rises from $18.50 to $19.75, effective 2026-01-01. Existing associates below $19.75 will be moved up on the same date.

## Phoenix Retention Bonus

Because of uncertainty about the Phoenix site, the committee approved a retention bonus of $1,500 for supervisors and lead associates at WH-PHX-01 who remain employed on 2026-06-30. Communication to eligible staff will be handled by Ava and Priya once the operations plan is approved. The reason for the bonus must not be shared with staff.
