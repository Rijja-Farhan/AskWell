## Our Commitment

Nimbus Logistics moves goods for thousands of customers every day, and they trust us to act honestly. This Code of Business Conduct (HR-POL-003) applies to every employee, contractor and temporary worker, at every site. It is also shared publicly so customers and suppliers know what to expect from us.

## Gifts and Entertainment

Employees may accept modest gifts and hospitality from customers or suppliers if the value is $100 or less per person per year from any single company. Gifts above $100 must be politely declined or reported to Legal and Compliance, who will decide whether the gift can be kept, donated or returned. Cash, gift cards and payments of any kind are never acceptable, whatever the amount.

Employees must never offer gifts or entertainment to win business improperly, and must never give anything of value to a government official without written approval from Legal.

## Conflicts of Interest

A conflict of interest exists when a personal interest could influence a business decision. Examples include hiring a relative, owning shares in a carrier we contract with, or working a second job for a competitor. Employees must disclose a potential conflict in writing within 30 days of becoming aware of it, using the Conflict Disclosure form. Disclosed conflicts are usually manageable; hidden ones are not.

## Fair Treatment and Safety

We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination or retaliation. Every employee has the right to a safe workplace and the duty to follow safety rules, including the Warehouse Safety Policy for anyone working on a warehouse floor.

## Speaking Up

Employees who see something that may break this Code should raise it with their manager, HR or Legal. Concerns can also be reported anonymously through the Nimbus Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-0142, available 24 hours a day, or online at ethics.nimbus.example. Nimbus strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Consequences

Breaking this Code can lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or of a contractor's engagement. Some breaches may also be reported to law enforcement. Every employee must complete Code of Conduct training each year by March 31.
