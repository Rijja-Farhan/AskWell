## Your First Week

### When do I get my badge and laptop?

Office employees collect their badge and laptop from reception on the first day at 9:00. Warehouse employees collect their badge from the site security office at the start of their first shift. Bring a photo ID.

### What training do I need to complete?

All new hires complete Code of Conduct training and security awareness training in their first 14 days. Warehouse employees also complete a site safety induction before working on the floor, and forklift operators must hold a valid Nimbus forklift certification.

## Health and Insurance Benefits

### When do my benefits start?

Medical, dental and vision coverage starts on the first day of the month after your start date. If you start on the 1st of a month, coverage starts that day.

### Which medical plans can I choose?

Nimbus offers two medical plans: the Nimbus Core PPO and the Nimbus HSA Plan. The HSA Plan has a lower premium and a higher deductible, and Nimbus contributes $750 per year to your health savings account ($1,500 for family coverage).

### When is open enrollment?

Open enrollment runs from November 1 to November 15 each year. Outside that window you can only change plans after a qualifying life event, such as marriage or the birth of a child.

## Retirement and Wellbeing

### Is there a 401(k) match?

Yes. Nimbus matches 100% of your 401(k) contributions up to 4% of eligible pay. The match vests immediately. You are automatically enrolled at 3% after 30 days unless you choose otherwise.

### Is there an employee assistance program?

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers 6 free confidential counseling sessions per issue per year, plus legal and financial advice. It is available to employees and their household members 24 hours a day.

## Pay and Time Off

### When is my first paycheck?

Nimbus pays every other Friday. Your first paycheck arrives on the first payday after you complete one full pay period. Details are in the Payroll and Leave FAQ on the intranet.

### How much PTO do I get?

Full-time employees receive paid time off and sick leave as set out in the Paid Time Off Policy. New employees can use PTO after their first 30 days.
