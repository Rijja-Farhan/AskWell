## Contacting the Service Desk

### What are the Service Desk hours?

The Service Desk is staffed Monday to Friday from 7:00 to 19:00 Central Time. Outside those hours, an on-call engineer handles urgent issues that stop a site from shipping orders or that affect security. The phone line is always open.

### How do I contact the Service Desk?

Call extension 4357 (it spells HELP) from any Nimbus phone, or 1-800-555-0177 from outside. You can also raise a ticket in the Service Portal at help.nimbus.example or chat with the Service Desk in Teams.

## Passwords and Accounts

### How do I reset my password?

Use self-service password reset at reset.nimbus.example. You will need your Nimbus Authenticator app. If you cannot use self-service, call the Service Desk; agents will verify your identity with a callback to your registered phone. The Service Desk will never ask you for your current password.

### My account is locked. What should I do?

Accounts unlock automatically after a waiting period, or you can unlock your account immediately using self-service reset.

## Tickets and Response Times

### How quickly will my ticket be handled?

Tickets are prioritized by business impact. The targets for a first response are:

| Priority | Example | First response target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | A site cannot ship orders | 1 hour |
| P2 | A team cannot work | 4 hours |
| P3 | One person cannot work | 1 business day |
| P4 | Question or request | 3 business days |

### Can I escalate a ticket?

Yes. If a ticket misses its target, reply to the ticket email with "escalate" in the subject. The Service Desk team lead will review it within 2 business hours.

## Hardware and Software

### How do I request new software?

Install approved software yourself from the Company Portal. If the software is not listed, raise a software request ticket; IT Security reviews new software within 5 business days.

### My laptop is broken. Will I get a loan device?

Yes. Each site has loan laptops. A loan device can be ready within 4 hours for P2 and P3 hardware tickets.
