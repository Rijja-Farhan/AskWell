## Purpose

This runbook tells the IT Security team and on-call engineers how to respond to security incidents such as malware infections, compromised accounts and data breaches. It is confidential because it describes detection tools and escalation contacts.

## Severity Levels

| Severity | Example | Response time | Incident commander |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEV1 | Ransomware, active data breach, WMS outage caused by attack | 15 minutes | CISO or deputy |
| SEV2 | Compromised admin account, malware on a server | 1 hour | Security Lead |
| SEV3 | Compromised user account, malware on one laptop | 4 hours | On-call engineer |
| SEV4 | Phishing email reported, policy violation | 1 business day | Service Desk |
| SEV4 | Lost device without evidence of misuse | 1 business day | Service Desk |

## Phases of Response

1. Detect and triage: confirm the alert and assign a severity.
2. Contain: isolate affected devices from the network and disable compromised accounts.
3. Eradicate: remove malware, close the entry point and reset credentials.
4. Recover: restore systems from clean backups and monitor for 72 hours.
5. Lessons learned: hold a review within 5 business days of closing a SEV1 or SEV2 incident.

## Ransomware Alerts

An alert with code NX-9001 from the endpoint detection and response tool means possible ransomware behavior. Treat every NX-9001 alert as SEV1 until proven otherwise. Isolate the device immediately using the EDR console; do not power it off, because memory evidence would be lost.

## Notifications

- The CISO must be informed of every SEV1 and SEV2 incident by phone, not email.
- Legal must be notified within 24 hours of any suspected breach of personal or customer data.
- Customer notifications are approved by Legal and the COO only.
- The Board is briefed by the CISO within 72 hours of a confirmed data breach.

## Evidence Handling

Collect logs, disk images and memory captures before rebuilding systems. Store evidence in the restricted evidence share and record a chain of custody for each item. Evidence is kept for at least 2 years.
