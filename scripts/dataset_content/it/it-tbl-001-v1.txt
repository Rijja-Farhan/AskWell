| Code | System | Meaning | First fix | Escalate to |
| NX-2101 | Email | Mailbox full (50 GB quota reached) | Empty Deleted Items and archive old mail | Service Desk |
| NX-2107 | Email | Calendar sync failure | Remove and re-add the account | Service Desk |
| NX-2150 | Email | Shared mailbox access denied | Request access with manager approval | Service Desk |
| NX-3301 | WMS scanner | Cannot connect to WMS | Check Wi-Fi and sign in again | Site supervisor |
| NX-3305 | WMS scanner | Barcode read failure | Clean scanner window and rescan | Site supervisor |
| NX-3310 | WMS scanner | App version mismatch | Update WMS app from Company Portal | IT Field Services |
| NX-4012 | VPN | Device certificate expired | Renew certificate in Company Portal | Service Desk |
| NX-4013 | VPN | MFA request timed out | Approve push within 60 seconds | Service Desk |
| NX-4020 | VPN | Split tunnel conflict | Disconnect other VPN or proxy tools | IT Infrastructure |
| NX-4031 | VPN | Account locked | Wait 30 minutes or use self-service reset | Service Desk |
| NX-5002 | Label printing | Print queue stuck | Restart the label print service | IT Field Services |
| NX-5010 | Badge printer | Badge printer offline | Power cycle the printer | Site security |
| NX-9001 | EDR | Possible ransomware behavior | Do not power off; call the Service Desk immediately | IT Security (SEV1) |
