## Meeting Details

Date: 2025-10-20. Chair: Ava Chen (Operations Manager). Attendees: Elena Ruiz (Shift Supervisor, WH-DAL-02), site manager for WH-DAL-01, transportation planning lead, Marcus Lee (IT). Weekly review of the two Dallas sites.

## Performance Last Week

WH-DAL-01 shipped 41,200 orders with an on-time rate of 96.2%, above the 95% target. Dock-to-stock time averaged 5.1 hours.

WH-DAL-02 shipped 28,900 orders with an on-time rate of 91.4%, below target. The main cause was a 3-hour conveyor control outage on 2025-10-14 that delayed the evening cut-off. Dock-to-stock time averaged 7.8 hours, above the 6-hour target, because of a backlog after the outage.

## Issues

- Dock door 7 at WH-DAL-02 has a damaged leveler and is out of service. Repair is booked for 2025-10-27. Until then, inbound appointments are reduced by four per day.
- Several scanners at WH-DAL-02 show error NX-3305 when reading labels from one supplier. The supplier's labels are printed too small. Purchasing will contact the supplier.
- Two near misses involving pallet jacks were reported at WH-DAL-01. Both were in the same aisle, where the walkway marking has faded.

## Peak Season Staffing

Peak starts on November 20. WH-DAL-01 will add 18 temporary associates and WH-DAL-02 will add 12. Temporary associates start on 2025-11-10 to allow time for safety induction and scanner training. Overtime plans will be shared with associates by 2025-11-13.

## Action Items

- Elena: recover WH-DAL-02 on-time rate to 95% by the end of October.
- WH-DAL-01 site manager: repaint the walkway markings in aisle 14 by 2025-10-24.
- Marcus: confirm conveyor firmware changes go through the Change Advisory Board.
- Transportation lead: review the evening cut-off at WH-DAL-02 with SwiftFreight.
