Customer Delivery and Tracking FAQ

Document code: OPS-FAQ-002 · Version 1 · Effective date: 2024-11-01 · Owner: Customer Experience · Classification: Public

Tracking Your Shipment

What does a Nimbus tracking number look like?

Nimbus tracking numbers start with NMB followed by 10 digits, for example NMB0123456789. Enter the number on the tracking page at track.nimbus.example to see the latest status.

How often is tracking updated?

Tracking is updated each time your shipment is scanned at a Nimbus site or by the delivery driver, usually at least once a day.

Delivery Windows

When will my order be delivered?

Standard deliveries arrive between 8:00 and 18:00 on business days. Business customers can book a 2-hour delivery window for an extra fee. Delivery dates shown on the tracking page are estimates until the shipment is out for delivery.

What happens if I am not home?

If a delivery fails, the driver leaves a notice and we try again on the next two business days. After three failed attempts, the shipment is held at the local depot for 7 calendar days for collection. Uncollected shipments are returned to the sender.

Damage and Claims

My shipment arrived damaged. What should I do?

Take photos of the packaging and the damaged items, keep all packaging, and file a claim within 15 calendar days of delivery through the claims form on our website. Claims filed after 15 days cannot be accepted. We aim to decide on claims within 10 business days.

Can I get a refund for a late delivery?

Refunds for late deliveries depend on the service purchased. Guaranteed services are refunded if delivered late; standard services are not.
