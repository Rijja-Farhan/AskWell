# Inbound Receiving Procedure

_Document code: OPS-MAN-001 · Version 1 · Effective date: 2024-09-15 · Owner: Operations Excellence · Classification: Internal_

## Purpose

This standard operating procedure describes how inbound trailers are received at every Nimbus distribution center, from dock appointment to putaway. It applies to all sites, including WH-DAL-01, WH-DAL-02, WH-PHX-01, WH-ATL-01, WH-CHI-03 and WH-SEA-01.

## Before the Trailer Arrives

Suppliers and carriers must book a dock appointment at least 24 hours in advance through the dock scheduling portal. The receiving lead reviews the next day's appointments each afternoon and checks that an advance shipping notice (ASN) exists in the WMS for each one.

## Checking In a Trailer

1. Confirm the appointment and trailer number at the gate.
2. Record the seal number and compare it with the number on the ASN. If the seal is broken or the number does not match, do not open the trailer: call the shift supervisor.
3. Assign a dock door and chock the trailer wheels before unloading.

## Unloading and Counting

Scan every pallet label with the handheld scanner. The WMS compares received quantities with the ASN. If the difference is more than 2% of units, put the whole shipment on hold, photograph the load and report the discrepancy to the supplier within 24 hours. Differences of 2% or less are received as counted and adjusted automatically.

## Damaged Goods

Damaged cartons or pallets are photographed, labeled with a red damage tag and moved to quarantine area Q1. Inventory in Q1 is not available for orders. The claims team reviews quarantined items within 2 business days and decides whether to return, rework or dispose of them.

## Putaway

Received pallets must be put away within 4 hours of unloading. The WMS assigns the storage location; associates scan the location barcode to confirm. Temperature-sensitive goods have priority and must be put away within 1 hour.

## Performance Targets

Sites aim to unload and check in a full trailer within 90 minutes of its appointment time. Dock-to-stock time, measured from trailer arrival to completed putaway, should average less than 6 hours. Site managers review these measures in the weekly operations review.
