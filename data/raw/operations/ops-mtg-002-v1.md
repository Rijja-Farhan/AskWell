# Network Consolidation Planning Meeting

_Document code: OPS-MTG-002 · Version 1 · Effective date: 2025-08-12 · Owner: COO Office · Classification: Confidential_

## Meeting Details

Date: 2025-08-12. Chair: Dana Whitfield (COO). Attendees: Ava Chen (Operations Manager), Leo Brandt (Finance Controller), Jordan Price (HR Director). Confidential: these notes must not be shared beyond the attendees until the announcement date.

## Proposal

Operations proposed closing the Phoenix distribution center, WH-PHX-01, by the end of Q3 2026. Phoenix volume has fallen 22% since 2023 and the site lease ends in September 2026. Its volume would move to WH-DAL-02 and to the new Savannah site, WH-SAV-01, which opens in March 2026.

## Financial Impact

Leo presented the business case. Closing WH-PHX-01 saves about $6.8 million per year in lease and operating costs from 2027. One-time costs are estimated at $3.1 million, including a severance budget of $1.2 million, equipment moves and early lease exit fees. Payback is expected in under 6 months after closure.

## People Impact

About 160 employees work at WH-PHX-01. All will be offered transfers to other sites where possible, with relocation support. Jordan confirmed that a retention bonus will be offered to key supervisors so the site runs well until closure. Severance will follow the standard plan: two weeks of pay per year of service, with a minimum of four weeks.

## Decisions

- The COO approved moving the proposal to the Board for final approval in November 2025.
- Communication embargo: no information about the closure may be shared with Phoenix staff, customers or carriers before 2026-01-15.
- Customers served from Phoenix will be told about the change by their account managers after the staff announcement.
