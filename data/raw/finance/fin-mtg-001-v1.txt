Meeting Details
---------------

Date: 2025-10-28. Attendees: Board Finance Committee members, the CEO, the CFO, Dana Whitfield (COO) and Leo Brandt (Finance Controller, secretary). Restricted: material non-public information. Do not share outside the attendee list.

Q3 2025 Results
---------------

Revenue for the first nine months of 2025 was $412 million, up 9% on the same period of 2024. Q3 revenue alone was $146 million. EBITDA margin for the year to date is 9.8%, compared with a budget of 10.5%. The gap is mainly due to higher temporary labor costs and the conveyor outage at WH-DAL-02.

Cash at the end of Q3 was $58 million. Capital expenditure is forecast at $34 million for the full year, mainly for the new Savannah site, WH-SAV-01.

Project Falcon
--------------

The CFO presented Project Falcon, the proposed acquisition of a regional trucking company with about 300 tractors in the Southeast. The proposed purchase price is $38 million, funded with cash and the existing credit facility. Due diligence will finish by 2025-12-15. The committee supports the deal subject to due diligence results and will recommend it to the full Board in January 2026.

Financing
---------

The committee approved extending the revolving credit facility from $75 million to $100 million, with the same banks, to fund Project Falcon and peak season working capital.

Phoenix Closure
---------------

The committee reviewed the business case for closing WH-PHX-01 and agreed to recommend it to the Board. Expected annual savings are $6.8 million from 2027.

Other Decisions
---------------

- No dividend will be paid for 2025.
- The 2026 budget will be presented to the committee on 2025-12-09.
- The external auditor will begin year-end fieldwork in January 2026.
