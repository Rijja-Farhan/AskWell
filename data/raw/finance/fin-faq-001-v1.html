Expense Reimbursement FAQ

Document code: FIN-FAQ-001 · Version 1 · Effective date: 2025-07-01 · Owner: Finance Operations · Classification: Internal

Submitting Expenses

How do I submit an expense claim?

Submit all claims in Concur. Photograph receipts with the Concur mobile app or forward them to receipts@nimbus.example, then attach them to your claim. Your manager approves the claim, and Finance checks it before payment.

When will I be reimbursed?

Approved claims are paid with the next payroll, usually within 5 to 7 business days of final approval. You can see the status of each claim in Concur.

What if I lost a receipt?

If you lose a receipt, complete a Missing Receipt Affidavit in Concur. Affidavits are accepted for expenses up to $75. For larger amounts, ask the supplier for a copy of the receipt.

Coding and Corporate Cards

Which GL account should I use?

Concur selects the account automatically from the expense type. Airfare and hotels use GL-6105, meals use GL-6110, and mileage and ground transport use GL-6115. Training courses use GL-6400.

Should I use my corporate card or my own card?

Use your corporate card for all travel bookings and business expenses where possible. Corporate card transactions appear in Concur automatically and must be matched with receipts within 30 days. Never use the corporate card for personal expenses, even if you plan to repay them.

Rules and Limits

Where are the limits for hotels and meals?

Limits are set in the Travel and Expense Policy. Claims above the limits are returned unless a manager approved the exception in advance.

Can I claim expenses for a customer dinner?

Yes, if the customer dinner was approved in advance by a director. List the names and companies of all attendees in the claim.
