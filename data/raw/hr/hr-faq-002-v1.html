Payroll and Leave FAQ

Document code: HR-FAQ-002 · Version 1 · Effective date: 2025-03-01 · Owner: Payroll Team · Classification: Internal

Payroll Basics

How often am I paid?

All employees are paid biweekly, every other Friday. When a payday falls on a bank holiday, pay is deposited on the previous business day.

Where do I find my payslip?

Payslips are available in Workday under Pay > Payslips from the Wednesday before each payday. Paper payslips are not issued.

How do I change my direct deposit account?

Update your bank details in Workday under Pay > Payment Elections. Changes made after the Monday of a pay week take effect in the following pay cycle, so your next payment may still go to the old account.

Overtime and Shift Pay

How is overtime paid?

Non-exempt employees are paid 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a work week. Work on a company holiday at a warehouse is paid at 2 times the regular rate. Overtime must be approved in advance by your supervisor in Kronos.

Is there a shift differential?

Yes. Second shift earns an extra $1.25 per hour and third shift earns an extra $2.00 per hour.

Requesting Leave

How do I request PTO?

Open Workday, go to Time Off, choose the dates and the PTO type, and submit. Your manager receives a notification. Requests longer than 3 consecutive days must be made at least 2 weeks in advance, as set out in HR-POL-002.

Where can I see my PTO balance?

Your current PTO and sick leave balances are shown in Workday under Time Off > Balances. Balances update after each payroll run.

Who do I contact about leave of absence?

Medical, military and parental leaves are handled by the Leave Team at leave@nimbus.example. Tell your manager as early as possible.

Tax Documents

When will I receive my W-2?

W-2 forms are available in Workday by January 31 each year. Paper copies are mailed only to employees who opted out of electronic delivery.

How do I change my tax withholding?

Submit a new W-4 in Workday under Pay > Withholding Elections. Changes apply from the next payroll run.

Need more help?

Open a ticket on the Service Portal or call the help line at extension 4357.
