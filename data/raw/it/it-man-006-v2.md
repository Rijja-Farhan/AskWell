# Handheld Scanner User Manual

_Document code: IT-MAN-006 · Version 2 · Effective date: 2025-06-01 · Owner: IT Field Services · Classification: Internal_

## Overview

Warehouse associates use Zebra TC58 handheld scanners to receive, pick, pack and ship orders in the warehouse management system (WMS). This manual explains daily use, basic fixes and the scanner error codes. It applies to all distribution centers.

## Start of Shift

1. Take a fully charged scanner from the charging cradle and check the battery health on the home screen.
2. Sign in to the WMS app with your badge number and PIN.
3. Confirm the scanner is connected to the Wi-Fi network NIMBUS-WMS-5G.

Replace any battery whose health is below 80%. Put the old battery in the red bin next to the cradle and log it with your supervisor.

## Resetting a Frozen Scanner

If the scanner freezes, perform a warm reset by holding the power button and the volume-up button together for 15 seconds until the screen goes dark. The scanner restarts in about 1 minute and keeps your data. Never remove the battery while the WMS app is saving.

## Scanner Error Codes

### NX-3301: Cannot connect to WMS

The scanner cannot reach the WMS server. Check that Wi-Fi shows NIMBUS-WMS-5G, move closer to an access point and sign in again. If the error continues, the device may need to be re-enrolled with StageNow by a supervisor.

### NX-3305: Barcode read failure

The scanner cannot read the barcode. Clean the scanner window with a dry microfiber cloth, hold the scanner 10 to 30 cm from the label and try again. Damaged labels must be reprinted at the nearest label station.

### NX-3310: App version mismatch

The WMS app on the scanner is older than the server version. Open the Company Portal on the scanner and update the WMS app. Do not continue scanning until the update is complete.

## End of Shift

Sign out of the WMS app and return the scanner to a cradle slot. Report damaged scanners to your supervisor, who raises a ticket. This manual is document IT-MAN-006 and replaces all earlier versions.
